The priority loop — visualized

The three interventions

Intervention Thesis Lead measure (30d) Payback Kill criterion
1. Tighten ICP, retarget paid Cut paid channels feeding non-ICP traffic. Redirect $30K/month to highest-activating sources. Activation rate +6–8 pts 2 quarters Activation flat at 60 days
2. Redefine activation event + rebuild onboarding Current activation event ("3 reports in 14 days") does not correlate with 90-day retention. Pick the event that does, rebuild the first 14 days around it. New activation event hit-rate > 40% 2 quarters New event uncorrelated with retention by week 6
3. Build customer-marketing loop No customer-marketing program today. Newsletter + advisory board + case studies feed Loop C (refs per customer). Product newsletter open rate > 40% 3 quarters Newsletter open < 30% at 60d

What this audit does not recommend

  • Cutting top-of-funnel spend reactively — hides the activation/retention problem for another quarter.
  • Launching a new acquisition channel — adds load to a broken downstream funnel.
  • Replacing the head of growth — the data does not point at a people problem yet.
  • Promising the board a one-quarter fix — activation and retention interventions are 2–3 quarter arcs.
