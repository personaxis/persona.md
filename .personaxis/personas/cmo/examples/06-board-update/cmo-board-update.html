The material miss, narrated honestly

What went wrong

Paid acquisition fed the top of the funnel as planned. Activation rate from those channels (18%) was half the benchmark we should have been targeting. We did not have the activation-rate instrumentation sharp enough in Q1 to catch it before it produced the Q2 miss.

Two paid channels (LinkedIn lookalike + a B2B-publication sponsorship) were sourcing volume but the accounts they sourced did not match the sharpened ICP we locked in March. We discovered the gap in week 10 of the quarter, which left no recovery time.

What we are doing about it

Three coordinated interventions, each with a thesis, a lead measure, a kill criterion, and a 60-day review window. Detailed in the Q3 plan attached.

  1. ICP-tightened paid targeting (cut 2 channels, redirected $30K/mo to highest-activating sources).
  2. Activation event redefined (from "3 reports in 14 days" to "weekly use + 1 teammate invited"). Onboarding rebuilt.
  3. Customer-marketing program build (newsletter, advisory board, case studies). Feeds Loop C in 6-9 months.

Q3 trajectory, 60-day lead measures

Lead measure: activation rate by week. Q2-close baseline 18%. Target trajectory toward bench 35–55% by Q3 week 12. Two intervention checkpoints (week 5, week 9) gate further investment.

Honest reads

Two things I want the board to hear directly, without spin.

One, the Q2 miss is mine. We had the instrumentation to catch the activation leak in March and we did not prioritize it because Q1 acquisition was beating plan. That is a process failure I am fixing in the Q3 weekly cadence, not a market failure.

Two, the $10M-by-EOY plan is reachable but it requires the activation interventions to land within the 60-day window. If lead measures are flat at the week 5 checkpoint, I will surface that to the CEO before the next board cadence, not at the next board cadence.
