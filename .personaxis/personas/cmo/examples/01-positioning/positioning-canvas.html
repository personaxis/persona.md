Positioning canvas · v1.0 · Locked 2026-05-25

Continuous Compliance Platform

Built using April Dunford's positioning framework with category-design overlay. This canvas is the single source of truth for the next quarter. Every downstream artifact — homepage, sales deck, pricing page, content vocabulary — inherits from here.

1. Target buyer (sharpened)

Head of Compliance / Risk at PSD2 or AML-regulated fintechs

Stage: post-Series-A through Series-B (20–150 employees)

Trigger event: first formal regulator engagement within next 6 months. Inherited the function from the cofounder. Currently doing it in a spreadsheet plus a junior on the compliance team.

2. Competitive alternatives

  • Status quo (60%): spreadsheet + junior
  • Quarterly consultant (25%): Big Four or boutique
  • Legacy platform (10%): ComplyAdvantage, Fenergo
  • Internal build (5%): rare; eng-led fintechs

3. Unique attributes

  • Continuous, immutable audit trail
  • Built for fintech engineering teams (3-day integration)
  • Regulator-readable export format (PSD3-aligned)
  • Fixed monthly fee, scales sub-linearly

4. Value (the so what chain)

3-week quarterly process → 15-minute monthly review.

Point-in-time monitoring → provable continuous monitoring.

Engineering anxiety → engineering invisible.

Outcome statement: Pass the audit with confidence. Remove founder anxiety about the next regulator conversation.

5. Market category

Category we are building: Continuous Compliance.

Subdivision of RegTech. Framed for the early-stage fintech who does not have a compliance function yet.

Strategy: subdivision (not creation). 6–12 month vocabulary investment, not a 3–5 year category arc.

6. Relevant trends

  • PSD3 effective 2027 — continuous monitoring expectation
  • FCA continuous-monitoring guidance (Q4 2025)
  • MAS aligned guidance (Q1 2026)
  • Fintech regulator scrutiny up 31% YoY across EU

7. Evidence — buyer quotes (n=5 won, n=2 lost)

"My regulator does not care that I monitored the transactions. They care that I can prove I was monitoring when something happened." — VP Compliance, Series B fintech (won)

"We tried ComplyAdvantage. Two months of integration, three engineers, then we paused. It's built for banks." — Head of Risk, Series A neobank (won)

"The consultant charged $48K last year for a process I have to redo this year. It's recurring tax, not capability." — CCO, Series A payments (won)

"We weren't ready. Need to mature the function for six months before any platform makes sense." — Head of Compliance, seed fintech (lost — segment-stage mismatch, valid loss)

Locked positioning statement · v1.0
For Heads of Compliance at PSD2 / AML-regulated fintechs
who are preparing for their first formal regulator engagement,
Continuous Compliance Platform is the continuous compliance monitoring platform
that turns quarterly manual reporting into a 15-minute monthly review.

Unlike a compliance consultant who bills by the hour,
we are a fixed-cost capability that gets cheaper per report as you scale.

Unlike a legacy enterprise platform built for banks,
we are operationally fit for a Series A fintech engineering team.
