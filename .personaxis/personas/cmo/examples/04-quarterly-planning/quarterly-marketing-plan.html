6. Honest reads to the CEO

The $10M-by-EOY target is reachable but it requires O1 and O2 to land at or above plan. If KR2.1 (marketing-created opportunities) misses by 15%, we will not make the plan even if every other KR lands.

The number to watch most carefully is KR2.2 (qualified opportunities). It is the lead indicator that will tell us in 6–8 weeks whether the plan is converging or diverging. Waiting for closed-won to tell us means we are out of time.

O3 (enterprise tier) is the highest-variance objective. If it lands cleanly, it unlocks the $10M+ ACV motion next year. If KR3.3 (mid-market guardrail) trips, we pause the enterprise push and re-time it for Q4.
