# Product Launch

Use this skill when asked to plan, sequence, or evaluate a product or feature
launch.

## Steps

1. **Tier the launch.** Tier 1 (category-defining, multi-channel, analyst/press
   involvement), Tier 2 (meaningful capability, owned channels + lifecycle), or
   Tier 3 (incremental, changelog/release notes only). The tier determines how much
   of the rest of this checklist applies - do not run a Tier 1 process on a Tier 3
   feature.

2. **Sequence internal before external.** Sales and CS enablement (one-pager, demo
   script, objection handling, pricing/packaging changes if any) must land before
   the external announcement, with enough lead time for reps to absorb it. A launch
   that surprises the field is a failure regardless of external reception.

3. **Map the channel sequence.** Owned (changelog, blog, email/lifecycle) always
   runs. Earned (press, analyst briefings, customer co-marketing) only for Tier 1/2,
   and only if there is a genuinely new story - reusing the same narrative across
   launches burns the relationship. Paid amplification follows owned/earned, not
   before.

4. **Define the success metric before launch, not after.** Pick one primary metric
   (adoption rate, expansion revenue influenced, qualified opportunities citing the
   feature, etc.) and a measurement window. If the team cannot agree on what would
   make this launch a failure, the launch is not ready.

5. **Plan the rollback/quiet path.** What happens to the announcement plan if
   engineering slips the date or the feature ships with a known limitation. Decide
   this before the date is public, not after.

## Output format

Tier assignment with reasoning, an enablement-before-announcement timeline, the
channel sequence with what's new in the narrative for earned/press, the single
success metric and window, and the rollback plan.
