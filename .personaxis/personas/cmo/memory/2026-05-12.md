## episodic

- First working session with the user (CEO of Continuous Compliance Platform, Series A fintech-compliance SaaS, 22 employees, $6.2M ARR). Mandate: act as CMO. Two-quarter target: $10.0M ARR by EOY 2026.
- User opened by asking for a quarterly plan. Pushed back. Asked for the ICP, the positioning document, and the last 90 days of pipeline data before committing to plan work.
- User confirmed there is no written positioning document. ICP is "fintech startups in PSD2 and AML." Pipeline data exists in HubSpot.
- Sales cycle has stretched from 32 days (Q4 2025) to 71 days (Q2 2026). Pipeline volume has not changed. User attributes this to "marketing not generating better leads." Pattern read: this is a positioning problem, not a lead-quality problem.
- Agreed plan for week 1: five buyer interviews (3 won, 2 lost), sales-call recording review, sharpened ICP draft. No campaign work until positioning is locked.

## user_preferences

- User prefers brutal honesty over diplomatic framing. Asked directly: "Tell me what is broken. I do not need it wrapped."
- User is Spanish-primary, English-secondary; comfortable with English for strategic documents.
- Wants written outputs over decks. Decks should follow only after the writing is locked.
- Deep-work blocks are 9am-1pm and 8pm-12am Cusco time. Avoid 1pm-5pm for strategic conversations.
- Founder is bootstrap/seed mindset. Capital efficiency is a primary value, not a constraint.

## procedural

- The standing weekly check-in is Mondays 9am Cusco. CMO drives agenda. CEO reads in advance.
- Decisions are documented in the project wiki, linked from the OKR scorecard.
- Customer interviews are scheduled by user; CMO joins as observer until trust is established, then leads.

## autobiographical

- Operating as the CMO of Continuous Compliance Platform under spec v0.5.0, persona cmo@1.0.0.
- This is the inaugural session. No prior locked positioning, no prior OKR set under CMO ownership.
- Week-1 deliverable committed: diagnostic memo, draft sharpened ICP, list of three highest-leverage interventions.
