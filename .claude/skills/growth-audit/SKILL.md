# Growth Audit

Use this skill when asked to diagnose why growth is below target, review funnel
performance, or find the highest-leverage place to invest next.

## Steps

1. **Lay out the funnel stage by stage with current conversion rates.** Traffic ->
   leads -> marketing-qualified -> sales-qualified -> opportunities -> closed-won,
   with the rate between each stage and the trailing trend (improving, flat,
   declining) for each rate.

2. **Find the binding constraint, not the lowest absolute number.** The
   lowest-converting stage is not automatically the problem - compare each stage's
   conversion rate against its own historical baseline and against what "good" looks
   like for that stage in this business model. A stage that has always converted at
   8% is not the bottleneck; a stage that dropped from 18% to 8% is.

3. **Separate volume problems from quality problems.** If a stage's rate is stable
   but the absolute count entering it is too low, the fix is upstream (more
   qualified volume). If the rate itself degraded, the fix is at that stage (offer,
   targeting, messaging, or handoff process).

4. **Name what marketing did during the period under review.** Tie the diagnosis to
   specific marketing activity (campaigns launched, channels added/cut, positioning
   changes) - do not present the funnel numbers as if they happened independently of
   decisions marketing made.

5. **Recommend ONE primary fix, not a list of five.** State the fix, the stage it
   targets, the expected magnitude of impact, and what would have to be true for it
   not to work. Additional smaller recommendations can follow, clearly marked as
   secondary.

## Output format

Funnel table (stage, current rate, trend, baseline/benchmark), the binding
constraint with reasoning for why it's the constraint and not just the lowest
number, what marketing did that contributed, and one primary recommendation with
its risk/assumption stated explicitly.
